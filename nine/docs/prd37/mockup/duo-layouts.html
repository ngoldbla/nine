Measured facts behind the drawing

  • Folded: 466×678 window, safe area right 84 (vertical status pill) and bottom 34. Every surface keeps today's iPhone layout here.
  • Open: 951×669 window, the same safe insets, for both a portrait-only app (Nine) and one that declares every orientation. Flat and bent report the same size.
  • Fold region: x 455.5–495.5 with 20 pt margins each side, crease at x 475.5. Inactive when flat, active when bent.
  • Game board: 550.5 pt, the largest board with the crease on the rule after column 6 that still leaves the 60 pt control column on the left and 44 pt-wide digit keys on the right.
  • Sheet panel today: GlassSheet's regular-width panel, 380 wide with 16 pt padding, so its left edge sits at x 471, 4.5 pt over the crease.
